Common Questions

How do fixed-price projects work?
We agree on scope before anything starts. You get a clear deliverables list, a fixed price, and a timeline. No hourly billing, no scope creep surprises.
What if my project doesn't fit a listed package?
Most projects do — but if yours is unusual, book a free AI audit. We'll scope it together and give you a custom quote within 24 hours.
How fast can you deliver?
Most setup projects (websites, AI agents) deliver within 1–2 weeks. Larger bundles take 2–4 weeks. Timeline is always confirmed upfront.
Do you work with non-technical founders?
Yes — most clients are non-technical. I handle the infrastructure, integrations, and AI layer. You stay focused on your business.
What does the 90-day guarantee mean?
If we don't hit the growth milestones we set together in 90 days, I keep working at no extra charge until we get there.
Can I start with a consultation and upgrade later?
Absolutely. Many clients book a 30-minute session first to map the strategy, then move into a project. Consultation fees are credited toward any project.
